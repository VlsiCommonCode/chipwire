Open ASIC

The OpenLane path to GDS.

Most open tapeouts do not start from a commercial P&R seat. They start from RTL that already simulates, then a PDK-constrained flow.

The sequence

  1. RTL lint and simulation against a golden model.
  2. Synthesis (typically Yosys) into the target PDK standard cells.
  3. Floorplan, power grid, placement.
  4. Clock tree and routing (OpenROAD).
  5. STA, DRC, LVS — fix, don’t wish.
  6. GDS signoff into Caravel, OpenMPW, or a foundry shuttle.

SKY130 and GF180 are the PDKs with the most public collateral. Timing closure on those nodes is a design constraint, not a tool bug: pipelines, memories, and I/O ring must be sized for the library you actually have.
