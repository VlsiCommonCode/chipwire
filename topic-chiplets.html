Packaging

Chiplets and 2.5D/3D as an SoC decision.

A chiplet is not a smaller SoC. It is a die with a die-to-die contract — UCIe, Bunch of Wires, or a proprietary PHY — plus a packaging and test story.

For open silicon, the useful questions are: which protocol, which bump map, who owns repair and known-good-die, and whether the interconnect looks like a bus, a NoC, or a memory-side port (HBM).

Research generators (Chipyard, Rocket) can model multi-tile systems long before a commercial UCIe PHY is in the repo. Treat those as architecture experiments unless the PHY, package, and DFT are specified.

Follow Chiplet news and the project database (tag: Chiplet) for implementations that actually expose a D2D interface.
